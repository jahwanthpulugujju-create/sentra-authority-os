# Reference Direction Notes

## Aura Auralis and VoxAura

Both Aura references use a decisive first viewport, an intentional focal object, tokenized component geometry, compact technical labels, and restrained ambient motion. Auralis pairs a bright background with a single focused interface path, while VoxAura uses atmospheric depth as a supporting layer rather than as page decoration.

### Transferable to Sentra

Sentra can adopt a stronger focal object in the hero, a more deliberate typographic scale, compact technical metadata, and a consistent interaction language. Motion should reveal state changes, proof transitions, or capability scope, rather than add generic ambient effects.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not adopt their indigo/cyan AI palette, dark atmospheric mode, audio-specific visuals, or generic AI product copy. The original Sentra light dashboard remains unchanged while this research is completed.

## Aura PeakPath and Beauty Studio

PeakPath reinforces the value of one large emotional statement, a carefully chosen display typeface, and layered ambient depth that remains subordinate to the content. Beauty Studio demonstrates a clear premium conversion path: a branded hero, a focused primary action, confidence-building proof, and a simple contact or next step.

### Transferable to Sentra

For Sentra, this translates to a clearer first screen, one primary judge action, larger intentional type, and a calmer proof section that supports the decision rather than competing with it. The site can also benefit from a stronger sequence from claim, to interaction, to evidence, to repository and demo resources.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not use dark green ambient effects, wellness imagery, beauty-service cards, gallery presentation, or a generic booking funnel.

## Secfi and Stripe

Secfi and Stripe demonstrate how high-stakes products establish confidence through clear product categorization, a single primary promise, practical evidence, and layered paths for distinct users. Their strongest transferable idea is not their visual palette; it is the progressive disclosure from headline, to proof, to deeper operational detail.

### Transferable to Sentra

Sentra should foreground one authority claim, make its primary live demonstration easy to enter, then let judges move from summary to decision details to proof records without cognitive overload. A compact, evidence-led metrics strip and clear technical paths can support this flow.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not imitate financial-services claims, use financial metrics as decorative proof, mimic Stripe’s gradient language, or present any invented customer quotations, ratings, or social proof.

## Wise and Ramp

Wise demonstrates the value of plain-language reassurance and a practical path from promise to safety explanation. Ramp reinforces an operational product posture: concise outcomes, clear workflow entry points, and interfaces that help a viewer understand what happens next.

### Transferable to Sentra

Sentra can improve its existing dashboard by making the live demo path more obvious, translating policy states into concise human explanations, and separating a quick judge view from inspectable proof details. The original layout already has a strong live state panel; future refinement should increase its clarity rather than replace it.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not borrow Wise green, currency motifs, banking reassurance language, Ramp payment concepts, or misleading business-scale metrics.

## Algorand and Highnote

Algorand’s case study emphasizes a unified brand system that still serves different audiences, while Highnote demonstrates a layered platform narrative: one foundation, clear product components, industry context, and a developer-facing proof path.

### Transferable to Sentra

The Sentra dashboard can evolve as one coherent story for judges, builders, and security reviewers. It should make the authority kernel feel like the shared foundation, with live demo, proof records, architecture, and repository guidance as connected but distinct paths.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not borrow blockchain imagery, virtual-card visual language, financial product claims, customer logos, endorsements, or invented partner quotations.

## Arc and Caribou

Arc uses a concise primary promise followed by decisive actions, while Caribou builds confidence through a clear ownership statement and an explicit explanation of how the service takes work from the user. Both maintain a disciplined progression from statement, to process, to deeper context.

### Transferable to Sentra

Sentra should articulate one simple ownership boundary: the agent proposes but Sentra owns the permission decision. The product should then present the judge with a clear action, the visible decision process, and the proof it leaves behind. This aligns with the original dashboard’s functional layout and can guide a future refinement without replacing it.

### Excluded from Sentra

Sentra will not borrow cash-management language, consulting-service imagery, finance-team customer claims, or any company logos and testimonials from the references.

## Consolidated direction

The appropriate future direction is a **bright, trustworthy, product-first authority interface**. It should preserve the original Sentra dashboard’s working live controls and proof-chain detail, while borrowing only these structural ideas: a stronger first-screen promise, one obvious demo action, a visible system artifact, progressive technical disclosure, and a quiet sequence from decision to evidence. It should explicitly avoid generic AI aesthetics, dark cyberpunk treatments, decorative 3D, gradients used as style filler, financial-product imitation, and unverified social proof.
