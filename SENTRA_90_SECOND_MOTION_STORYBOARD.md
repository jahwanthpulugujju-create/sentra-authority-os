# Sentra 90-Second Motion-Graphics Storyboard

## Production brief

The video is a **90-second, landscape, software-only SaaS motion-graphics film** for the final round. It should use Sentra’s pearl-white background, ink-navy structure, oxblood decision boundary, verified green, and blue evidence signals. The visual language is a calm vector/3D hybrid: data paths, capability tokens, verification gates, policy rails, and hash-linked evidence. Avoid hardware, people, generic cyberpunk, dark backdrops, invented customer logos, and dense on-screen copy.

> **Core line:** “The model can change its reasoning. It cannot change its authority.”

## Nine-shot sequence

## Generation notes

Generate nine coherent 10-second landscape clips, using the same reference frame and brand colors. Prefer slow camera drift, graceful path animation, and clean material transitions; do not use fast cuts, faces, product screenshots with unreadable text, or claims beyond the controlled MVP. Compose the clips in order with a short 6-frame dissolve or boundary-line wipe between adjacent shots. A restrained, original electronic underscore may be added only if it does not compete with live narration.
